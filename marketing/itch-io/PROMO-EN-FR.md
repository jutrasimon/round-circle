## itch.io devlog — body

The Round Circle browser prototype is ready to explore.

This is a compact experiment in survival strategy and philosophical storytelling. You recruit defenders from houses, choose bonuses, and control the simulation through the convoy's pace. Between waves, a conversation asks who a place really belongs to.

A Witness run has five minutes of simulation time, with pauses for dialogue and decisions. There are four recruit profiles, ten waves, different endings, and a visual report of your run.

I've also left the Dev Lab open. You can explore visual atmospheres, spawn creatures, and experiment with the systems. Those changes affect the run: consider it an invitation into the workshop.

It's still a prototype, and I'd love focused feedback:

- Was recruitment clear on your first try?
- At what wave did the pressure feel right—or unfair?
- Did the conversations change how you understood the village?

### One-sentence pitch

Keep a train and its village alive through ten waves, while a stranger challenges what it means to call somewhere home.

### Thirty-second pitch

Round Circle is a small survival strategy game on a circular island. Recruit four very different defenders, pick bonuses, and change the simulation's speed through the train's pace. As the vortex grows and the waves intensify, brief conversations shift the question from how to survive to what you are protecting. Different choices can lead to different endings—including a more harmonious resolution.
