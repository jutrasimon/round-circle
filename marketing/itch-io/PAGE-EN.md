## Description — copier à partir d'ici

### What makes a place home?

A train circles a village. A vortex grows at its heart. Beyond the tracks, something is getting closer.

Recruit your defenders, keep the convoy alive, and face ten escalating waves. Between battles, listen to a stranger who remembers this place differently.

**Perhaps home is something we share.**

### A small world under pressure

- **Four distinct recruits.** Choose a Guardian, Watcher, Gunner, or Scout from each house. Recruitment takes time. Decide who you need next.
- **Ten waves. One shrinking sense of safety.** Combat happens automatically; your choices shape who survives.
- **Set the pace.** The train's speed control changes the pace of the entire simulation. Slow down, speed up, or pause.
- **Words that matter.** Brief philosophical conversations explore belonging, ownership, and the meaning of home. Your choices can lead to different endings.
- **See the story of your run.** Explore health curves, damage charts, wave history, and a detailed final report.
- **Open the Dev Lab.** Explore the game's working parts: visual atmospheres, enemies, settings, and experiments. The workshop is part of the experience.

### How to play

1. Press **Start** when you're ready.
2. Click a house or its **+** icon and choose a soldier. Watch the recruitment bar above the building.
3. Choose a bonus after clearing a wave.
4. Use **Convoy pace** to control game speed, or **Pause** to take a breath.
5. Read the conversations and choose your replies. Dialogue and reward selection pause the action.

Drag the scene to orbit the camera. Scroll to zoom. Settings are available before you start; the Dev Lab is available during play.

### Before you enter the circle

**Free browser prototype · Single player · English · Mouse and keyboard recommended**

A Witness run contains five minutes of simulation time. Reading, pauses, and your chosen speed change the actual session length.

Round Circle is in development. Expect experiments and evolving balance. Dev Lab changes can affect your current run. Browser settings are remembered locally; an unfinished run is not saved for later.

The game uses grain, flashes, and camera effects. Visual settings can be explored in the Dev Lab.

### Tell me about your run

What felt clear? When did the pressure become too much? What did “home” mean to you by the end?

If something breaks, please include your browser, the wave number, and whether you changed any Dev Lab settings.
